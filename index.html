How to Play

Steer & Throttle Use A / D or Left / Right arrows (or swipe / touch buttons on mobile) to change lanes. Hold W / Up to push top speed, S / Down to brake, and Space for Nitro boost.
Protect Your Cargo Hard braking at high speed, blasting through puddles, and scraping cars all damage Cargo Integrity. A direct crash or 0% cargo ends the run!
Monsoon Weather Rain makes asphalt reflective and slippery—lane changes drift wider and braking takes longer. Fog shortens visibility. Upgrade Rain Tires in the Garage!
Close Calls = Big Cash Thread tight gaps near traffic without touching to trigger Close Call bonuses. Reach the glowing green Drop-Off gate before the timer expires!
